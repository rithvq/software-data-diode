## Implemented

- Authenticated raw/state binary parts with real 128-byte headers, complete-body digest, immutable identity and domain-separated Ed25519 binding to the locally approved gateway/key/epoch/contract. N3 JSON evidence parts remain unchanged.
- A durable, budgeted signed forward-state slot alongside immutable raw and evidence work. Serialized headers, signatures, UDP/IP model overhead and fixed repeats consume local byte grants. Underfunded combined budgets fail startup.
- Gateway UDP attempts independent of local send outcomes or Fog replies. Crash-ambiguous charged grants are retired as uncertain, never replayed for free.
- Passive bounded diode UDP forwarding; no transformation, receiver-response retry, Fog HTTP health poll or request/response metrics export in research mode.
- Bounded authenticated Fog reassembly and a separate, anchored SQLite reorder-aware replay history. Duplicates, identity/sequence forks, stale windows, unknown/retired epochs, storage limits and restart behavior are distinct from the legacy verifier.
- Bounded durable pending raw/state processing toward existing internal Fog services. Rule evidence stays in local replay retention; no N6 capsule/verdict PostgreSQL schema or publication guarantee is claimed.
- Fog-local per-device evidence age/staleness and forward-state age, without interpreting a heartbeat as no event.
- Explicit local readiness and feature/configuration checks, private non-root volumes, persistent device counters and a repeatable local verification utility.

Normative bounds, signature preimages, accounting, replay semantics and guarantees are in [N5-transport-v1.md](N5-transport-v1.md).

Within the **existing** contract tolerance, closure now defers until both clocks cross. Admission still checks UTC membership separately; larger jumps, regressions and closed-scope reopening remain refused. Early/late boundary tests and all earlier clock tests pass. Recorded interrupted/discontinuous historical windows were not rewritten.

Extended runs require reviewed cache/raw archival and retirement; pending work must not be silently discarded.
